Disclosures & Methodology

How the static ETF tools are sourced, dated, and calculated.

LAST UPDATED

November 2026

Educational use

These tools are for education and general information only. They are not personalized advice, a recommendation, or an offer to buy or sell any security. Results do not guarantee future performance, income, or outcomes.

Screener

The screener uses a fixed client-approved snapshot of 339 ETF records. Official issuer pages and issuer product lists are the primary sources. When an issuer does not publish a metric in the captured source, the field is shown as N/A rather than estimated.

The screener may show source dates and status labels such as closed, legacy, excluded ETN, or unverified. These labels are intended to make the snapshot transparent and do not represent a current investment recommendation.

Overlap

The SCHD versus VYMI overlap tool compares manually prepared holdings snapshots dated July 31, 2026. It uses country-aware ticker matching and normalized company-name cross-checking. Holdings and weights can change over time, and the result may differ from a live portfolio analysis.

Backtester

The backtester compares SCHD, JEPI, VYMI, and VOO using monthly static price observations. Observations are aligned by calendar month. Adjusted-close prices are used as a total-return proxy, not official total-return series.

The illustration assumes a starting amount and, where selected, a fixed monthly contribution. It does not model taxes, trading costs, bid-ask spreads, slippage, fund-specific distributions, investor behavior, or future contributions beyond the selected input. Results are hypothetical and do not represent an actual managed account.

Primary source websites

  • Vanguard ETF list
  • Schwab Asset Management SCHD page
  • J.P. Morgan Asset Management JEPI page
  • iShares / BlackRock product list
  • State Street SPDR ETF listing

Exchange listings may be used only to confirm that a ticker exists and is listed. They are not used as the primary source for issuer yield or expense-ratio metrics. Third-party sites are not used as the primary screener dataset.

Data updates

This is a manually updated static snapshot. Source dates are preserved with the relevant dataset, and the data may become outdated as issuers change holdings, fees, yields, fund names, or fund status.
