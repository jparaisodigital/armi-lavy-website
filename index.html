QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Quick answers before you get started.

Who is the First Dividend Paycheck guide for?

It is written for beginners who want a plain-English introduction to dividend investing, with no prior experience needed.

What is included in the guide?

It covers how dividend investing works, how to evaluate dividend safety, how to think about a starter portfolio, and how to build a practical investing routine. It comes as a PDF plus an interactive HTML version.

Is this financial advice?

No. Everything on this site is for educational purposes only and is not individualized financial, tax, or investment advice. Investing involves risk, including loss of principal.

How do I receive the guide after purchase?

Purchases are processed securely through Gumroad. After checkout, you get access to your download through Gumroad.

How accurate is the dividend calculator?

The calculator gives estimates based on the numbers you enter. Real dividends, prices, and taxes change over time, so results are not guaranteed.

Do I need a lot of money to start learning?

No. Learning the basics costs nothing, and the calculator lets you test small amounts. How much to invest, and whether to invest at all, depends on your own situation.
